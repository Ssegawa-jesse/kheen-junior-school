Who we are

Raising children on Deen, discipline and diligence.

Kheen Junior School nurtures every learner in Islamic values while building strong academic and practical foundations — so that what they carry out of our gates becomes a legacy that outlives the classroom.

Deen First

Daily Qur'an, Tajweed and Islamic studies woven into school life.

Discipline

Structured routines that build strong, respectful character.

Community

Beyond the classroom

Co-curricular & hands-on activities

Learning at Kheen doesn't stop at books. Our learners rotate through clubs and practical projects that build skill, confidence and teamwork.

01

Qur'an & Tajweed Circle

Daily memorisation and recitation practice in small, guided groups.

02

Arabic Language Club

Building reading, writing and conversation skills in Arabic.

03

Football & Athletics

Inter-house leagues and track events that build fitness and teamwork.

04

Debate & Public Speaking

Weekly debates that sharpen confidence, logic and articulate speech.

05

Art, Design & Calligraphy

Islamic calligraphy, pattern-drawing and craft projects.

06

Science & Robotics Club

Simple builds and experiments that turn curiosity into skill.

07

Nasheed Club

Vocal Islamic nasheeds that celebrate faith without instruments.

08

Gardening & Environment Club

Growing and tending real crops on the school's own plots.

The Kheen Bakery

Our learners run a real working bakery — measuring, mixing, baking and packaging their own bread, cakes and mandazi. It's mathematics, hygiene, teamwork and enterprise, all learned by doing.

Getting to school

Safe, supervised school transport

Kheen Junior School offers organised transport for learners across Kakiri Subcounty and surrounding areas, so parents can trust the journey as much as the classroom.

  • Covers Elite Village, Luwunga and nearby routes in Kakiri Subcounty
  • Supervised pick-up and drop-off with a dedicated attendant
  • Punctual, consistent timing for morning and evening trips
  • Route and fee enquiries handled directly by the school office

Ask about routes serving your area when you call the school office.
